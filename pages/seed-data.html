Seed Demo Data

Dev-only utility. Populates localStorage with sample patient (orders, addresses, prescriptions) and staff data so every screen has something to show. Safe to run multiple times — it resets first.

Quick links after seeding:

  • Homepage — click Sign In (top right) to pick a persona: Customer 1/2, Admin, Pharmacist, or Dispatch
  • My Account — orders, addresses, prescriptions (once signed in as a Customer)

Staff logins behind the persona picker: admin@medify.local / admin123 · pharmacist@medify.local / pharma123 · dispatch@medify.local / dispatch123

Note: orders, addresses and prescriptions are shared storage — the Customer 1 / Customer 2 personas will see this same seeded data, not separate accounts.

The 4-digit PIN gate (pages/login.html) is a separate MVP access boundary, unrelated to persona login.
